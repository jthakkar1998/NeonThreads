THE RAVE RULES

Six commandments of the T-shirt exchange. Read these before the draw.

6 GOLDEN RULES
01♢

One Person. One Shirt.

You'll be randomly assigned one person from the group. Your mission? Find them a hilarious T-shirt they'll have to rock at the pregame and concert.

02$

Budget: $25–$40

Keep your shirt within the $25–$40 range. You don't need to break the bank to make someone look ridiculous.

03♙

No Spoilers. No Swaps.

Keep your assignment a SECRET until the exchange! No telling anyone who you got, and absolutely no trading assignments.

04✦

Make It Personal

The best shirts are the ones that hit close to home. Inside jokes, embarrassing references, and friendly roasts are highly encouraged. Put some thought into it!

05✧

Pregame = Shirt Exchange

Bring your shirt to the pregame! That's when everyone will exchange shirts and find out who got them what.

06♧

You HAVE to Wear It

Whatever shirt you receive, you're wearing it to the concert. No exceptions. No backing out. Embrace the embarrassment.
